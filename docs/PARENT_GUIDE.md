## Getting Started

### Creating Your Parent Account

1. **Register**
   - Visit the platform
   - Click "Sign Up"
   - Select "Parent" as account type
   - Fill in your details
   - Create a strong password

2. **Link Your Child**
   - After registering, you can either:
     a) Create a student account directly linked to yours
     b) Have your child register and enter your username

3. **Verify Email** (if enabled)
   - Check your email for verification link
   - Click to verify your account

### Dashboard Overview

Your parent dashboard shows:
- List of your linked children
- Each child's progress overview
- Time spent today/this week
- Recent activities completed
- Quick access to controls

## Creating Accounts

### Option 1: Parent Creates Child Account

1. Log in as parent
2. Go to "My Children"
3. Click "Add Child"
4. Fill in:
   - Child's username
   - First name (can be a nickname)
   - Password (or auto-generate)
   - Date of birth (for age-appropriate content)
5. Click "Create Account"

### Option 2: Child Registers Independently

1. Your child visits the platform
2. Clicks "Sign Up"
3. Selects "Student"
4. Enters YOUR username in "Link to Parent Account"
5. You'll receive notification to approve

### What Information is Required?

**For Parent Account:**
- Your first name and last name
- Email address
- Username
- Password

**For Child Account:**
- First name (can use nickname)
- Date of birth
- Username (child-friendly)
- Password (simple to remember but secure)

**Privacy Note**: We collect minimal personal information. Birthdate is used only for age-appropriate content filtering.

## Monitoring Progress

### Viewing Child's Progress

1. Log in as parent
2. Click on your child's name
3. View progress dashboard showing:
   - Activities completed
   - Subjects explored
   - Current level and XP
   - Time spent
   - Badges earned

### Progress Reports

**Daily View**: Shows activities completed each day of the week

**Subject View**: Shows progress by subject (Math, Science, etc.)

**Activity History**: Complete list of all activities with:
- Date completed
- Score achieved
- Time spent
- Star rating

### Understanding the Metrics

- **Level**: Indicates overall progress (higher = more activities completed)
- **XP (Experience Points)**: Earned from completing activities
- **Coins**: Currency for avatar customization
- **Star Rating**: 1-5 stars based on score
- **Streak**: Consecutive days of activity

### Setting Goals

You can help your child set goals:
- "Complete 3 activities today"
- "Earn 100 coins this week"
- "Reach Level 5"
- "Master Math activities"

## Setting Parent Controls

### Time Limits

**Daily Time Limit**:
1. Go to child's profile
2. Find "Time Limits" section
3. Set daily limit in minutes
4. Options:
   - 30 minutes
   - 60 minutes
   - 90 minutes
   - 120 minutes
   - Custom amount
   - Unlimited

**Weekly Time Limit**:
- Same process as daily
- Prevents excessive use over the week

**What happens when limit is reached?**
- Child sees friendly message
- Access to activities is paused
- Can still view rewards and profile
- Resets at midnight

### Allowed Hours

Set specific times when your child can use the platform:

1. Go to "Schedule" in child's profile
2. Set "Allowed Hours Start" (e.g., 8:00 AM)
3. Set "Allowed Hours End" (e.g., 8:00 PM)
4. Click "Save"

**Example Schedule**:
- School days: 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM
- Weekends: 9:00 AM - 7:00 PM

### Blocked Days

Prevent access on certain days:
- School days during exam weeks
- Family vacation days
- Any day you prefer they don't use screen time

### Content Restrictions

**By Subject**:
- Allow/deny entire subjects
- Example: Focus on Math and Science only

**By Difficulty**:
- Set maximum difficulty level
- Prevents frustration from too-hard content

**How to Set**:
1. Go to child's profile
2. Find "Content Filters"
3. Check/uncheck allowed subjects
4. Set maximum difficulty
5. Save changes

### Notifications

Receive email updates about:
- When child completes activities
- When child reaches new level
- When child earns badges
- Daily/weekly activity summary

**To Enable**:
1. Go to your profile
2. Find "Notifications"
3. Check desired notifications
4. Save

## Understanding the Gamification

### Why Gamification?

Gamification makes learning fun by:
- Providing immediate rewards
- Creating sense of achievement
- Encouraging continued learning
- Building healthy competition (leaderboards)

### The Reward System

**XP (Experience Points)**:
- Earned from completing activities
- Determines user level
- Higher activities = more XP
- 50 XP = 1 level

**Coins**:
- Currency for avatar items
- Earned from completing activities
- Bonus for high scores
- 5-20 coins per activity (varies)

**Badges**:
- Achievements for specific accomplishments
- Examples:
  - "First Steps" - Complete 1 activity
  - "Explorer" - Complete 10 activities
  - "Streak Week" - 7 consecutive days
  - "Math Wizard" - Complete 10 math activities

**Avatar Items**:
- Unlockable clothing, hair, accessories
- Purchased with coins
- Some items require reaching certain levels
- Premium items for exceptional achievements

### Leaderboards

Weekly rankings showing:
- Top XP earners
- Top coin collectors
- Most activities completed

**Privacy**: Leaderboards use usernames only, not real names.

## Tips for Parents

### For Young Children (3-5 years)

- Start with lower difficulty activities
- Set shorter time limits (30 minutes max)
- Focus on subjects they enjoy
- Celebrate small achievements
- Do activities together
- Use larger font size (accessibility options)

### For School-Age Children (6-8 years)

- Encourage trying all subjects
- Set moderate time limits (60-90 minutes)
- Use progress to identify strengths/weaknesses
- Balance fun with learning
- Let them explore independently

### For Older Children (9-12 years)

- Set higher time limits if needed
- Encourage tackling harder activities
- Use streaks to build habits
- Discuss what they learned
- Connect activities to school work

### General Tips

1. **Set a Routine**
   - Same time each day
   - Consistent duration
   - Builds learning habit

2. **Balance Screen Time**
   - Mix learning with physical play
   - Platform time should be part of balanced day
   - Use time limits to enforce balance

3. **Engage with Learning**
   - Ask what they did today
   - Show interest in badges earned
   - Discuss difficult activities
   - Celebrate achievements together

4. **Use Progress Data**
   - Identify subjects they excel in
   - Notice areas needing support
   - Adjust content filters based on progress

5. **Encourage Independence**
   - Let them choose activities
   - Allow mistakes (learning opportunity)
   - Praise effort, not just results

6. **Monitor for Frustration**
   - If child consistently struggles
   - Lower difficulty level
   - Try different subject
   - Break into shorter sessions

## Troubleshooting

### Child Can't Log In

**Problem**: Invalid username/password

**Solutions**:
1. Check spelling of username
2. Reset password if forgotten
3. Ensure caps lock isn't on
4. Check account is active

### Time Limits Not Working

**Problem**: Child can still access after limit

**Solutions**:
1. Ensure limit is saved
2. Check browser cache
3. Log out and log back in
4. Contact support if issue persists

### Progress Not Showing

**Problem**: Activities not showing in progress

**Solutions**:
1. Ensure activities are fully completed
2. Check browser is updated
3. Try different browser
4. Clear browser cache

### Can't Set Parent Controls

**Problem**: Controls not saving

**Solutions**:
1. Verify you're logged in as parent
2. Ensure you're linked to the child's account
3. Check internet connection
4. Refresh page and try again

### Child Can't Access Certain Activities

**Problem**: Activities are blocked

**Solutions**:
1. Check content restrictions
2. Verify age-appropriate setting
3. Check difficulty level restriction
4. Ensure subject is allowed
